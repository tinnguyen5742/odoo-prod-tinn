Property Management in Odoo

Property Management Odoo App runs an entire real estate operation inside Odoo. Configure parent properties and auto-generate their floors and units for towers, classify each property as residential, commercial, industrial or land, attach amenities, nearby connectivity, parking spaces, land titles, facing direction and area details, then publish the catalogue on your website for prospects to browse and enquire.

On the back office, every inquiry can become a booking for sale or a tenancy for rent. A booking walks through Draft, Confirmed and Cancel with a single workflow ribbon, generates the customer invoice from a configurable product, prints the agreement PDF and tracks the agent commission as a fixed amount or a percentage of the price. A tenancy auto-creates a rent schedule with advance rent plus monthly installments, highlights due dates on a calendar, sends rent reminder mails on the configured cadence, and lets the team raise maintenance invoices straight from the tenancy.

Agreements come with their own expiry workflow, renewal and email reminders. Dedicated dashboards cover property analytics and booking analytics; portal access lets owners, tenants and agents see their own records, raise maintenance requests, view rent schedules and download documents. Built for property managers, real estate agencies, landlords and brokers who want one workflow for sale, rent, finance and customer self-service.

Property Website

The Property Website module lets you showcase properties online with advanced search and filtering capabilities. Prospective tenants and buyers can explore listings, view detailed information, and submit inquiries directly through a user-friendly interface.

Main Features:

  • Advanced filtering and search functionality for easy property discovery.
  • Detailed property display pages with high-quality images and full specifications.
  • Integrated web forms for seamless capturing and routing of customer inquiries.

Property Home Page

The home page opens with a Find Your Dream Property hero and search bar, then lays out Featured Properties, Browse By Property Type cards (Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Land), Properties for Sale and Properties for Rent sections, and a Why Choose Us strip at the bottom.


Buy or Rent Search

Visitors switch between Buy and Rent tabs on the hero, pick a city, a property type and an optional keyword, and hit Search to jump to a filtered listing page.


Filtered Property Listing

All Properties page lists matching results with active filter pills for property type, purpose, city and industrial type. Each card shows the photo, badge (For Sale or For Rent), price and city.


Refine and Sort Results

Use the Purpose dropdown (All, Buy, Rent) and Sort by (Newest First, Oldest First, Price Low to High, Price High to Low) to narrow the catalogue further.


Property Detail Page

Each property opens to a detail page with hero image, address, price, sticky tabs (Overview, Amenities, Specifications, Location, Price) and a Contact Seller button at the top right.


Overview, Amenities and Contact Seller

The detail page lists property type, purpose, built year, property age, amenities (24x7 Security, CCTV Surveillance, Parking and more), specifications, area and facing. A Contact Seller form on the right captures name, email, phone and message, with a Get Contact Details button and social share icons.

Further down, Room-wise Area, Location and Nearby (Bus Stop, Fuel Station, Hospital, School, Park), a Price Breakup with monthly rent and security deposit, and a Downloads block where the visitor can view or download the attached agreement.


Request Submitted

After submitting the contact form, the visitor sees a Thank You page with their reference number (for example REF/0003) and a note that the team will get back to them shortly.


Inquiry in Back Office

Every submitted request lands in the Inquiry menu as a lead with reference number, opportunity name, contact, email, salesperson, expected revenue and stage, plus quick Email and SMS buttons on every row.


Booking Dashboard

The Booking Dashboard offers an interactive, centralized view to effectively manage all your property reservations. It allows property managers to instantly assess booking availability, status, and duration from a unified interface.

Main Features:

  • Intuitive visual representation of all current, upcoming, and past property bookings.
  • Quick monitoring of booking states to easily avoid scheduling conflicts.
  • Streamlined tracking of reservation timelines for maximized property utilization.

Booking Dashboard Overview

The Booking Dashboard greets the user, then lists every main property with all its sub-properties as a grid of cells. Booked cells are highlighted in pink (BO) and unbooked stay grey (NB), with a Print button on the top-right.

Filter by Rent or Sell

The Property filter switches the grid between All Property, Rent or Sell so the team focuses on the properties they actually need to see.

Filter by Owner

Pick a specific owner from the Owner dropdown and the grid narrows to only that owner's properties (for example Lumber Inc).

Filter by Booking State

The Booking dropdown (All Booking, Booking, Unbooking) isolates just the booked cells when needed, useful for spotting occupied versus available stock at a glance.

Filter by Main Property

Zoom into a single tower or building (for example Vintage Flats) using the Main Property filter to see every sub-property cell underneath it.

Filter by Sub Property

Pick one specific unit from the Sub Property dropdown (for example Amazon Warehouse - T1 - P1) and the grid isolates just that unit and its booking state.

Full Booking Grid

Cleared of filters, the dashboard shows every main property and every sub-property cell across the portfolio, with owners and booking status visible in one scroll.

Analytics Dashboard

The Analytics Dashboard provides a comprehensive and highly visual overview of operations across your property portfolio. It highlights key metrics and trends in real-time, enabling property managers to make data-driven decisions that enhance profitability.

Main Features:

  • Real-time tracking of critical operational metrics and financial performance.
  • Comprehensive graphical charts focusing on property statuses, revenues, and statistics.

Property Dashboard

The Analytics Dashboard greets the user and stacks Owner, Customer, Property, Recidency and Date filters across the top. KPI tiles cover All Property, Total Lead and Opportunity, Total Owner and Tenancy, Booked Property (Rented, Salleble, All Booking) and All Payment (Due, Done, Total Payment). A Print button on the top right prints the full dashboard.


Lead Source & Booking Charts

A Lead Source bar chart shows where leads come from (Search engine, Lead Recall, Newsletter, Facebook) next to a Booking Deal Month Wise pie chart. Each chart has a download button to save it as a PNG, and clicking a slice drills into the matching records.

Property Type & Payment Charts

A Property Type chart breaks the portfolio by residential, commercial, industrial and land alongside an All Payment doughnut chart (draft vs posted). Each card has a Chart Type selector with Bar, Line, Pie and Doughnut options so managers pick the visualisation that suits them.

Upcoming Opportunity & Rent Pending

Two live tables sit side by side. Upcoming Opportunity shows name, customer, phone and date, while Rent Pending lists number, property, tenancy, amount and due date. A View button on every row jumps straight into the record.

The complete dashboard layout in one view: greeting, filters, KPI tiles, charts and the upcoming opportunity and rent pending tables stacked together.

Portal Management

Portal Management provides dedicated, self-service portals tailored for tenants, owners, and agents. Users can easily access, review, and print their property details, associated contracts, and relevant documentation whenever needed directly through the web portal.

Main Features:

  • Secure access allowing tenants, owners, and agents to view their respective lists and details.
  • Built-in capability to generate and download reports or documents natively as PDFs.

Tenant My Account

When a tenant logs in, the My Account page shows Sales Orders, Your Invoices, Addresses, Connection and Security cards alongside a dedicated Tenant Property card that opens their leased properties.


Tenant Property List

The portal lists every tenancy linked to the logged-in tenant with reference, property, type, owner, confirmation date and state badge. Confirmation, Filter By and Group By dropdowns sit on top for quick sorting.

Tenant Property Detail

A tenancy detail page in the portal shows property type, property, owner, tenant, dates, tenancy details (rent type, monthly rent, deposit payment, agent, start date, duration, end date) and the rent schedule with payment status. Download and Print buttons sit at the top.

Owner My Account

When an owner logs in, the My Account page exposes an Owner Property card alongside the standard portal cards, opening the full list of properties they own.

Owner Property Detail

Each owner property opens to a detail page with name, property type, parent property, owner, condition, address, property purpose, construct year, age, selling price, plus other details (residential type, bedrooms, bathrooms, parking, alarm). Download and Print buttons let the owner pull a PDF anytime.

Agent My Account

When a travel agent logs in, the My Account page adds an Agent Commission Property card so the agent can see and download every property they earned a commission on.

Agent Commission List

The agent's commission list shows reference, property, property price, commission type (Fixed or Percentage), commission amount and state (Draft or Done). Sort By and Group By controls help organise the list.

Lead Integration

Lead Integration effortlessly links property inquiries with your CRM or sales pipeline, ensuring that every opportunity is captured and tracked. This seamless workflow connects prospects to available properties quickly, preventing customer drop-off.

Main Features:

  • Automatic integration of website queries and portal requests as actionable system leads.
  • Centralized lead monitoring helps agents maintain engagement up to successful conclusion.

Website Inquiry Submitted

Visitors submit an inquiry from the public property page and see a Thank You screen with their reference number, while a fresh lead lands in the back office at the same time.

Inquiry List in Back Office

Every website inquiry lands in the Inquiry menu with reference number, opportunity name, contact, email, salesperson, expected revenue and stage, ready for the sales team to qualify and convert.

Book Property from Lead

The lead record adds a Book Property button at the top. One click generates the booking and a Booking smart button appears on the lead, so the inquiry stays linked to its booking from then on.

Property Booking Workflow

The Property Booking Workflow simplifies the end-to-end process of selecting and reserving properties for lease or purchase. Property managers and agents can view available properties, rapidly load the specifics, and draft precise booking commitments directly into the system.

Main Features:

  • Dedicated property booking interfaces providing rapid, structured request creation.
  • Functionality to browse and quickly load required properties into booking lines.
  • Track entire booking requests reliably from inception to agreement finalization.

Book Property Menu

The Sell Property menu exposes Book Property and Bookings entries. Book Property opens the booking request wizard so the team can reserve a property without leaving the inquiry pipeline.

Book Property Wizard

The Book Property wizard captures the property type, sub type, furnishing, minimum and maximum budget, total size and status. Click Load Properties to list every matching property, tick Select Property on the right row, then Make Booking Request to create the booking.

Bookings List

All booking requests live in the Bookings list with number, property, booked by, customer, booking date, ask price, confirmation date and status (New or Confirmed).

Dynamic Tower Configuration

The Dynamic Tower Configuration lets property managers automatically generate complete building hierarchies with a few clicks. Utilizing intuitive setup forms, you can rapidly model multi-story towers and distribute sub-properties consistently across every floor.

Main Features:

  • Instantly configure and generate sub-properties across designated building floors simultaneously.
  • Save substantial administrative time avoiding repetitive manual entries for structurally identical units.

Define Towers on a Parent Property

On the parent property, tick Tower and Generate Dynamical Tower, then add the tower tags (for example A-block, B-block, C-block). The Generate Tower button sits at the top, ready to build the structure.

Tower and Floor Plan

Generate Tower fills the Tower Details tab with every floor on every block (for example A-block Floor 1 with 3 units, Floor 2 with 2 units, B-block Floor 3 with 2 units). Click Generate Sub Properties to turn each unit into its own sub-property record.

Sub-Properties Generated

A Property smart button at the top of the parent counts every generated sub-property (24 in this example). Click it to open the list.

The Sub-Property list shows every unit individually (PROP/0052 to PROP/0075) named after the tower, floor and unit (for example Amazon Warehouse - A-block - F1 - U1) with status, monthly rent, owner, tenant and total size columns.

Commission Workflow

The Commission Workflow streamlines the way broker and agent remunerations are computed and paid. By automatically linking closed property transactions or leases to defined commission rules, managers can reliably calculate and issue accurate vendor bills on schedule.

Main Features:

  • Automatically compute appropriate commissions upon completed transactions and property agreements.
  • Seamless one-click generation of corresponding agent vendor bills in accounting.
  • Maintain transparent, auditable history of all owed and settled broker compensation.

Create Commission from Tenancy

On a running tenancy, the Create Commission button at the top opens the commission record. An Agent Commission smart button keeps the count visible so the team always sees how many commissions are linked to the tenancy.

Commission Record & Generate Bill

Each commission shows agent, rent or sell property, property price, commission type (Fixed or Percentage) and the calculated amount. Click Create Agent Commission Bill to raise a vendor bill payable to the agent.

Bill Linked to Commission

Once generated, a Bill smart button appears at the top of the commission record so the user opens the linked vendor bill in one click.

Tenant Workflow

The Tenant Workflow comprehensively coordinates all interactions throughout a tenant's lease tenure. Manage everything accurately from contract initiation and automated rent schedule generation to effortless tracking of deposits and ongoing rent collections.

Main Features:

  • Centralized dashboard presenting dedicated tenancy records organized efficiently.
  • Effortlessly configure custom rent schedules immediately converting signed terms to financial tracking.
  • Streamlined tools for receiving security deposits and applying partial settlement using the deposit balance.

Tenancy Kanban

The Tenancy menu opens a kanban of every active and confirmed tenancy with property, tenant and monthly rent on each card. A status sidebar groups them as All, Confirm or Running.

Generate Rent Schedule

A confirmed tenancy record holds property, owner, tenant, dates, rent type, monthly rent, security deposit, duration and agent. Click Generate Rent Schedule to build the full payment timeline based on these values.

Rent Schedule & Deposit Receipt

The Rent Schedule tab lists every installment with name, due date, rent amount, status and payment reference. Receive Deposit raises a deposit payment, then each line carries Receive Rent and Payment by deposit amount actions so the team can settle dues against the deposit when needed.

Deposit Balance Guard

If the team tries to settle a rent line with Payment by deposit amount when the deposit balance is too low, a Validation Error pops up with the message "You have insufficient deposit amount" so nothing slips through.

Agreement Workflow

The Agreement Workflow meticulously manages document generation and lifespan tracking for all your leased or sold properties. You can convert verified bookings into official digital agreements, while automated alerts ensure that upcoming expiry dates are never overlooked.

Main Features:

  • One-click conversion of finalized property bookings into formal sale or rent agreements.
  • Integrated views centralizing all agreements associated with a particular tenancy.
  • Proactive, automated email reminders dispatched prior to and directly on agreement expiration dates.

Generate Sell Agreement from Booking

A confirmed sale booking carries a Generate Sell Agreement button. One click drafts the agreement and adds an Agreement smart button at the top so the link stays visible from the booking record.

Sell Agreement List

The Agreements menu splits into Rent Agreement and Sell Agreement. The Sell Agreement list shows number, tenant or contact, property, agreement type, date, expiry date, expiry days countdown and status (Draft, Running, Cancelled or Expired) with a sidebar to filter by status.

Expiry Reminder Email

A scheduled task sends an advance email reminder ("Agreement is going to be Expire") to the manager, with a View Agreement button so they can renew in one click. The number of advance days is set in configuration.

Same Day Expiry Email

On the actual expiry date, a second email arrives ("Agreement has been Expired"), with View Agreement again so the team can move straight to renewal or close-out.

Rent Agreement Feature

Generate Rent Agreement Button

A running tenancy carries Generate Rent Agreement, Generate Maintenance Invoice, Close and Create Commission buttons at the top, with smart buttons for any agreement or maintenance invoice already linked to the record.

Rent Agreement Wizard

The Generate Rent Agreement wizard pre-fills tenant, property, owner, monthly rent, agreement date, duration and expiry date. Pick a saved Agreement Template (for example Property Agreement) and the body populates with reusable clauses. Click Generate to create the agreement record.

Agreement & Maintenance Invoice Linked

After generation, an Agreement smart button and a Maintenance Invoice smart button both appear at the top of the tenancy. The team opens either record directly without leaving the tenancy.

Maintenance Invoice Wizard

The wizard lists every maintenance line with a Generate Invoice checkbox per row, so the user picks which charges to bill. Click Generate Invoice to create the customer invoice linked back to the tenancy.

Customer Payments Menu

A dedicated Payments menu lists every customer payment (rent and maintenance) with date, number, journal, payment method, customer, amount and status (Posted or Draft).

From the tenancy gear menu, Tenancy Details prints a multi-page PDF with property, tenant, dates, rent details, the full rent schedule with statuses, and a maintenance breakdown.

Tenancy Analysis

Due Rent Report

Booking History Report

Reporting > Booking History opens a wizard with property type and date range. Print PDF generates a history list with booking number, property, date, booked by, contact, sell price, ask price and status.
