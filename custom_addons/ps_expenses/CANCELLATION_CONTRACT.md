# Trip cancellation — bounded local contract, 2026-10-20

Current PO approval continues D6 with whole-trip cancellation and retained obligations. This is delegated first-release workflow design, not an actual company's cancellation, eligibility, refund or accounting policy. D6 remains IN_PROGRESS; financial assurance NO_GO / REQUIRES_SPECIALIST_VERIFICATION.

Owner requests cancellation with reason, exact trip-preparation revision and cancellation revision. Current assigned HR manager decides approve/refuse with reason and exact cancellation revision; no self-approval/title-only authority. Draft/refused trips also use this explicit manager decision, not deletion. One pending request at a time; same exact replay is idempotent. Owner may withdraw a pending request before decision with reason. Refusal/withdrawal retains history and returns to no pending cancellation; a new request increments its own revision. Approved cancellation is terminal for travel; no reopen through draft/submit/revise. Already workflow-closed cases cannot be cancelled retroactively.

Cancellation is a separate axis, not a replacement of original business approval. The original business decision, submitted facts and receipts stay intact. Pending/approved cancellation blocks trip edits/new submissions and new advance intent/execution preparation. Existing unexecuted advances may be cancelled; no implicit cancellation or automatic no-payment declaration. An already-prepared uncertain advance/settlement must still be reconciled using its exact existing source/reference/amount; cancellation cannot convert uncertainty to zero or authorize a new transfer. Pending cancellation pauses new settlement approval/execution preparation/closure, but not reconciliation of an already-prepared operation. Existing settlement approval/locked bases and recorded actual cash are not erased or automatically reopened.

After manager approves cancellation of a previously approved trip, existing expense and settlement review remain available to resolve retained employee-paid costs and funding. Evidence/claim validity still requires finance decisions under configured policy. Explicit no-advance confirmation is still required where relevant; missing records are never assumed to prove zero. Cancellation itself never changes accepted costs, payment direction, outstanding amount, cash records, accounting status or workflow-closure flags.

The case remains open after cancellation until its separate supported settlement/closure gates are met. Even a pre-business cancellation is shown as travel stopped with separate financial status, not a verified zero/accounting-complete assertion. No zero-line settlement, external bank transfer, deduction, statutory fee, automatic reversal or journal entry is invented. Incomplete no-expense finance clearance and post-execution policy/authority amendment remain explicit release gaps rather than admin SQL edits.

All cancellation actions lock trip→advance→expense→settlement, then recheck current actor/revisions. Append-only trip events retain cancellation revision, reason, actor/time and source IDs/states/outcomes, without granting managers expense proof/amount access. Printed monetary summaries disclose the separate cancellation status. Source actors/assignments/policy remain subject to current controls; production use needs real policy/specialist verification, full UAT/recovery and fresh whole-product review/PO acceptance before deployment.
